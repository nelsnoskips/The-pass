# US Restaurant Software: Market Size, SaaS Unit Economics, Pricing Benchmarks and Funding (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-11. All dollar figures USD. "Est." = estimate; "Inference" = my calculation from cited figures. Where only aggregator sources were found, that is stated.

## 1. How many US restaurant locations are there (independent vs chain, FSR vs LSR), and how many open/close per year?

### Takeaway
Technomic counts ~412.5k independent locations (end-2025, down 2.3% YoY) and ~263k chain locations (up ~1.4%), i.e. ~675k "restaurant" locations; broader counts (Datassential, incl. more foodservice) put the US universe at 860k+. Independents are shrinking by roughly 9,500 net units a year, with full-service independents the weakest segment.

### Cited Findings
- Independents finished 2025 at **412,498** locations vs **422,001** in 2024, a ~2.3% decline (~9,500 net loss) (Technomic data, published early 2026) — [Restaurant Business](https://restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/number-independent-restaurants-declined-23-2025); [Nation's Restaurant News](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/u-s-restaurant-count-continues-to-fall)
- Chain locations rose ~1.4% to more than **263,000** units (Technomic, 2025) — [Restaurant Business](https://restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/number-independent-restaurants-declined-23-2025)
- Another Technomic-based report puts the independent decline at **2.5%** and says **full-service independents** were the weakest segment, down **2.6%** in 2025 — [FES Magazine](https://fesmag.com/topics/perspectives/this-week-in-foodservice/23426-a-changing-landscape-independents-contract-as-chains-rethink-operations)
- Top 500 chains grew units **1.5%** (~3,600 units) in 2025; **33** of the Top 500 closed 10%+ of locations in 2025 (vs 17 in 2023) (Technomic) — [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/top-500-restaurants/mass-closures-became-a-lot-more-common-last-year)
- Total US restaurant locations hit an all-time high of **860,000+** as of Nov 2025 (Datassential-based, broader definition) — [ScrapeHero](https://www.scrapehero.com/fast-food-and-restaurant-closures-in-us-store-closure-report)
- Orbital's directory counts **632,090** US locations in its restaurant universe (methodology differs) — [Orbital](https://www.withorbital.com/data/how-many-restaurants-in-the-us/)
- Toast estimates **207,800+** fast-food chain locations alone (2025 blog) — [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/zh-us/blog/on-the-line/how-many-restaurants-are-in-the-us)
- Datassential reported **886** total restaurant closings in April 2025 (single month); its first-year failure rate table shows a sharp decline (methodology based on review-site closure verification) — [Datassential](https://datassential.com/resource/restaurant-failure-rate/)
- NRA projects **$1.55T** restaurant & foodservice sales in 2026 (+4.8% nominal, +1.3% real), **15.8M** employees (Feb 2026) — [NRA](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/media/press-releases/persistent-cost-increases-and-enduring-demand-will-shape-the-restaurant-industry-in-2026/); [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/national-restaurant-association-15-trillion-restaurant-food-service-sales/812133/). BofA forecasts $1.54T (+4.3%) — [BofA](https://business.bofa.com/en-us/content/restaurant-industry-report.html)
- Nearly 10% of full-service restaurants face risk of closure in 2026 (study) — [PMQ](https://www.pmq.com/study-nearly-10-of-full-service-restaurants-face-risk-of-closure-in-2026/)

### Inferences
- **TAM (locations):** ~675k Technomic restaurant locations (412.5k indie + 263k chain). The Pass's core target (independents + small groups of 1–20 units) is roughly the 412.5k independents plus the small-chain tail; small groups of 2–20 units are counted partly as "independent" by Technomic (Technomic treats <10-unit concepts as independents, per its standard definition — verify).
- **SAM (inference):** If ~half of independents are full-service / sit-down (where reservations, deposits, guest CRM matter most), SAM is ~200k locations. This split is an assumption, NOT a sourced figure.
- **Dollar TAM (inference):** 412.5k indie locations x $6k/yr (=$499/mo) = ~$2.5B ARR; x $12k/yr (~$1,000/mo) = ~$5B.
- Net closure of ~9.5k independent units/yr is ~2.3% of the base; gross closures are much higher (gross openings/closures not found), so location churn from business failure is a structural floor for any SMB restaurant SaaS.

### Gaps
- No authoritative 2025/2026 split of independents by full-service vs limited-service count (only growth rates). NRA SOI 2026 does not give unit counts in the excerpts found.
- No reliable annual gross openings and closures (only net). Census CBP/BLS QCEW counts for NAICS 7225 were not pulled.
- No count of "small groups 2–20 units" specifically.

## 2. What do restaurants spend on technology, and what do they pay for point tools?

### Takeaway
No credible 2025–2026 "tech as % of revenue" figure was found. Published list prices suggest a 5–8 tool stack for a full-service independent easily runs $1,000–$2,500+/month before per-cover/per-order fees.

### Cited Findings
- 46% of operators said they're most likely to put tech dollars toward **digital marketing** (RB/NRN 2025 Technology Outlook, 550+ operators) — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/digital-marketing-remains-restaurants-top-tech-priority)
- 48% of QSR/fast-casual brands plan to increase tech investment in 2026; operators cite execution and **fragmentation** as bottlenecks (Qu 2026, 168 brands) — [Qu](https://www.qubeyond.com/resource-center/the-execution-gap-what-the-2026-restaurant-technology-benchmark-reveals-about-operator-priorities)
- 82% of operators are actively reducing expenses (Popmenu State of the Plate 2025, 300 operators, Aug–Sep 2025) — [Popmenu](https://get.popmenu.com/toolkit/state-of-the-plate-2025)
- **Reservations — OpenTable:** Basic $149/mo + $1.50/network cover; Core $299/mo + $1/network cover; Pro $499/mo + $1/network cover (checked Aug 2026 per third party) — [Eat App blog](https://restaurant.eatapp.co/blog/opentable-pricing). Capterra UK lists different tiers ($249/$449) — [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/17313/opentable-for-restaurants)
- **Reservations/CRM — SevenRooms:** unpublished; reported $499 to $700–$1,000/mo (unverified third-party estimates) — [Servme](https://www.servmeco.com/resources/sevenrooms-competitors)
- **Website/marketing — Popmenu:** Starter $179, Essentials $299, Premier $499/mo (annual prepay ~$159/$269/$449); extra location ~$300/mo, online ordering ~$50/mo/location, AI phone answering from $149/mo (aggregators, 2026) — [Restolabs](https://www.restolabs.com/blog/popmenu-pricing); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/popmenu); [Popmenu pricing page](https://get.popmenu.com/pricing)
- **Website/ordering/marketing — Owner.com:** Flexible $249/mo + 5% restaurant fee per order; Flat Rate $499/mo, no restaurant per-order fee; month-to-month; separate ~5% guest "order support fee" reported by a competitor — [Owner.com pricing](https://owner.com/pricing); [Sauce](https://www.getsauce.com/post/owner-com-pricing-fees)

### Inferences
- Illustrative stacked cost for a full-service independent (list prices, inference): OpenTable Core $299 + Popmenu Essentials $299 (+$50 ordering) + loyalty/SMS tool ~$100–300 + review mgmt ~$100–300 + AI phone $149 + ads agency/management $300–1,000 = **~$1,300–$2,400/mo**, plus OpenTable per-cover fees (e.g. 400 network covers x $1 = $400/mo). Loyalty, review, and ads-management prices are NOT sourced here; treat as placeholders to verify.
- A flat $499–$799/mo per-location all-in-one is credibly positioned below the stacked cost.

### Gaps
- No sourced figure for tech spend as % of revenue or $/location/month (NRA Restaurant Operations Report / Toast surveys not found in this pass).
- Loyalty (e.g., Thanx, Toast Loyalty), review management (Birdeye/Podium), and Toast Marketing add-on prices not verified.

## 3. Public benchmarks for restaurant SaaS and SMB vertical SaaS unit economics

### Takeaway
Toast: ~189k locations, $2.5B ARR (subscription ARR $1.28B) at Q3 2026, implying ~$565/mo subscription ARR per location and ~$1,100/mo total; SaaS NRR 109% (2025); subscription gross margin ~79% GAAP. Olo ARPU ~$955/quarter. Owner.com est. ~$100M ARR mid-2026. General SMB SaaS benchmarks: ~70–80% gross margin, 12–24-month CAC payback, monthly churn 1.5–3% healthy.

### Cited Findings
- **Toast Q3 2026 (quarter ended Sep 30, 2026):** ~**189,000** locations (+21% YoY), ~**8,500** net adds in Q3; ARR **$2,503M** (+24%), Subscription ARR **$1,279M** (+28%), Payments ARR $1,224M (+21%); subscription revenue $317M in Q3; GPV $61.8B (+20%); subscription GAAP gross profit $250M on $317M (~79%; non-GAAP ~83%); FY2026 adj. EBITDA guide $840–850M. ARPU and NRR not disclosed in the release — [Toast 8-K Ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001650164/000165016426000214/tost-20260930xexhibit991.htm)
- **Toast 2025:** 164,000 locations at year-end, **30,000** net adds in 2025; SaaS **net retention 109%** for 2025; management expects mid-single-digit SaaS ARPU growth in 2026; Q4 2025 GPV per location -1% YoY — [Motley Fool transcript, Q4 2025](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/12/toast-tost-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript); [Yahoo/MarketBeat highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/toast-q4-earnings-call-highlights-101233713.html)

ARR ~$34M end-2024, ~$81M end-2025 (+137%), ~**$100M** by July 2026; ACV moved from ~$6K to ~$10K as it moved into multi-unit (estimates, not audited) — [Sacra research](https://sacra.com/research/hubspotification-of-mom-pop-restaurants/); [Sacra profile](https://sacra.com/c/owner); Latka reported $20M ARR (Mar 2024, interview) — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/blog/how-owner-com-grew-to-20m-arr-the-revenue-journey/)

### Inferences
- Toast subscription ARR per location = $1,279M / 189k = **~$6,770/yr (~$564/mo)**; total ARR per location = $2,503M / 189k = **~$13,240/yr (~$1,100/mo)** (inference).
- Olo ARPU ~$955/quarter = **~$318/mo** per location (inference; Olo is mostly enterprise chains).
- Owner.com at ~$100M ARR and ~$6–10K ACV implies roughly 10–15k paying locations (inference).
- Toast SaaS NRR of 109% with heavy location growth suggests gross location churn is meaningful but offset by upsell; no company-reported churn rate found.
- For The Pass plan: model 75–80% gross margin target, 2–3% monthly logo churn (SMB restaurant; business failure alone ~2–3%/yr net for independents), CAC payback target <12 months given founder-led network sales.

## 4. Pricing models and price points of comparable all-in-one offerings

### Takeaway
The market anchors around **$249–$499/mo per location** for website/ordering/marketing bundles (Owner, Popmenu), **$149–$499/mo + per-cover** for reservations (OpenTable), and SevenRooms at an unverified $499–$1,000/mo. Owner's $499 flat, month-to-month plan is the closest model to The Pass.

### Cited Findings
- Owner.com: $249/mo + 5% per order, or **$499/mo flat** with no restaurant per-order fee; month-to-month; guest-side 5% order support fee reported — [Owner.com](https://owner.com/pricing); [Sauce](https://www.getsauce.com/post/owner-com-pricing-fees)
- Popmenu: $179 / $299 / $499 per month; extra location ~$300/mo — [Restolabs](https://www.restolabs.com/blog/popmenu-pricing); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/popmenu)
- OpenTable: $149 / $299 / $499 per month + $1–$1.50 per network cover — [Eat App](https://restaurant.eatapp.co/blog/opentable-pricing)
- SevenRooms: unpublished; reported $499–$1,000/mo — [Eat App comparison](https://restaurant.eatapp.co/blog/best-restaurant-reservation-software-apps-in-united-states-2026); [Servme](https://www.servmeco.com/resources/sevenrooms-competitors)
- Sacra describes Owner's flat $499 subscription vs order commissions as its growth model — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/owner/?highlight=owner.com)

### Inferences
- A flat price above $499 needs to replace reservations (OpenTable/SevenRooms) as well as website/ordering to justify itself; a $599–$899 tier that clearly removes per-cover and per-order fees is defensible against a $1,300+ stack.

### Gaps
- Toast bundle pricing (Toast Marketing, Loyalty, Online Ordering add-ons) not verified this pass.
- "Open" (getopen.com, LA, founded 2019) pricing and funding not found.
- Resy (Amex) pricing not checked.

## 6. Variable costs that matter for gross margin

### Takeaway
SMS is the biggest controllable COGS item: ~$0.012–0.013 all-in per US 10DLC segment. Card processing is passed through or margin-generating. LLM cost per agent run is cents or less with current models.

### Cited Findings
- **Twilio SMS:** $0.0083/segment base (US long code) — [Twilio pricing](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing); carrier pass-through per outbound segment: AT&T ~$0.0035, Verizon ~$0.005, T-Mobile raised 10DLC SMS from $0.0030 to **$0.0045** in Jan 2026 — [Twilio support](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/44609260499995-T-Mobile-Messaging-Carrier-Fee-Changes-January-2026); realistic all-in ~$0.012–0.013/segment; phone number ~$1.15/mo; failed message fee $0.001; 10DLC brand registration one-time ~$4–46, campaign vetting ~$15, campaign fee ~$10–15/mo (third-party summaries; verify) — [Kanal](https://getkanal.com/blog/twilio-sms-pricing); [Telnyx](https://telnyx.com/resources/twilio-telnyx-sms-pricing)
- **Stripe US:** 2.9% + 30¢ online card; 2.7% + 5¢ in-person (Terminal); manually entered cards +0.5% (verified Sep 2026 by third party) — [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/us/pricing); [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/learn/stripe-fees)
- **LLM API (Anthropic list prices, per 1M tokens input/output, docs cached 2026-11-03):** Claude Haiku 5.5 $0.10/$0.50; Claude Sonnet 5.5 $2/$10; Claude Opus 5.5 $4/$20; prompt cache reads ~0.1x input price — [Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)

### Inferences
- **SMS:** 2,000 guest SMS/location/month x $0.0125 = **~$25/mo** + ~$1–15 in number/campaign fees. A 10,000-message campaign month is ~$125. Cap or meter SMS volume in the flat plan.
- **LLM per agent run (inference):** a run of 20k input + 2k output tokens costs ~$0.003 on Haiku 5.5, ~$0.06 on Sonnet 5.5, ~$0.12 on Opus 5.5 (before caching). At 300 runs/location/month on Sonnet that is ~$18/mo; with caching, lower.
- **Deposits/gift cards via Stripe Connect:** processing can be passed through to the restaurant or marked up (application fee) as a margin lever, consistent with Toast's fintech-heavy model (Payments ARR ~49% of Toast ARR).
- Illustrative COGS per location at $499–$799/mo: SMS $25–60 + LLM $10–40 + email ~$5 + hosting/DB ~$5–15 + support time = target 75–85% gross margin (inference; email and hosting not sourced).

### Gaps
- Email cost per 1k (Postmark/SendGrid/Resend) not verified this pass. Typical list: about $1 per 1,000 at volume, but no source collected.
- Hosting (Netlify/Neon) per-tenant costs not researched.
- Twilio 10DLC fee schedule not read from Twilio's own A2P article; figures are from third-party summaries and conflict.
