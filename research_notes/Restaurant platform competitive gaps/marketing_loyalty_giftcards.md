# US Restaurant Guest Marketing: SMS/Email, Loyalty, Gift Cards (as of Nov 2026)

Research scope note: 15 tool calls. Coverage is strongest on Toast, Square, Punchh/Thanx/Paytronix, Owner.com/Popmenu/SevenRooms, 10DLC fees, loyalty dissatisfaction surveys and vendor case studies. Coverage is weak or missing on Fishbowl, Incentivio, Spendgo, Como, Bloom Intelligence, Klaviyo/Mailchimp restaurant pricing, Yiftee/Giftfly, and on Reddit operator threads, which searches did not surface. Those are listed under Gaps. Most pricing below comes from third-party blogs, not vendor price pages, and the blogs often disagree.

## Leaders: what each does best and what it charges

### Takeaway
The market splits in two. Enterprise loyalty/CRM (Punchh, Thanx, Paytronix) is quote-only, often $400 to $5,000+/month on multi-year contracts, and needs marketing staff. POS-bundled tools (Toast, Square) are cheap and month-to-month but lightweight and locked to their POS. Website-led platforms (Owner.com, Popmenu) sit at roughly $179 to $499/month flat. No public price list combines reservations, POS visit data, loyalty, gift cards and SMS for an independent at a flat rate.

### Inferences
- A single independent paying Toast (about $185, plus IQ Grow at $699 if it wants AI) or Owner.com ($499) is already near or above a plausible flat per-location fee for The Pass. That leaves room for a bundle that includes reservations.
- Square's per-text overage (1.5 to 3¢) is a visible, operator-understood unit cost. The Pass's flat pricing needs a stated fair-use text allowance, or an "SMS at cost" line, to avoid margin risk from Twilio per-segment fees plus carrier surcharges.
- 10DLC registration itself is cheap (under $70 upfront), but the time (up to 5 business days, with rejections) and the paperwork are the pain points. A platform that files brand and campaign registration on the operator's behalf, through Twilio ISV/subaccounts, removes a real setup hurdle.

- Square gift card fees (eGift and physical card prices) not confirmed. The restaurant pricing page did not show them.
- Toast IQ Grow pricing (the $499 to $699 change) rests on a source that returned 403. It was seen only in search snippets.

## Operator complaints

### Takeaway
The best-documented complaints are dissatisfaction with loyalty programs (37% dissatisfied in an RB/NRN survey), programs that are too basic or discount-heavy, no ROI measurement, POS lock-in, and enterprise cost and staffing burden. Reddit-specific operator threads did not surface in search, so first-person complaint evidence is thin.

### Cited Findings
- 37% of operators said they were dissatisfied with their current loyalty program. Only 19% were very satisfied. Many brands revamping loyalty aim to reduce the discount they give out — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/why-so-many-restaurant-chains-are-revamping-their-loyalty-programs)
- Bikky 2026 survey of 50+ multi-unit operators: 82% run standard points programs, only 16% have tiered or status mechanics, and 39% say their structure is too basic to differentiate (the second most common complaint) — [Bikky 2026 Loyalty Sentiment Survey](https://bikky.com/blog/bikkys-2026-loyalty-sentiment-survey) (vendor survey)
- 40% of restaurant brands do zero ROI measurement on loyalty — via [search summary of PepperHQ State of Loyalty](https://www.pepperhq.com/blog/state-of-loyalty-report-2025) (attribution uncertain; report date unclear)
- Consultant view: target discounts of 1 to 2%. Giving away around 10% "does not add up" — [NRN, Why restaurant loyalty programs fail](https://www.nrn.com/expert-opinions/why-restaurant-loyalty-programs-fail) (opinion)
- Value-menu discounting is undermining loyalty programs — [QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/value-incentives-are-undermining-restaurant-loyalty-programs/)
- Recognition, not discounts, is the perk most restaurants are not offering. Some customers do not want discounts — [Restaurant Dive (sponsored)](https://www.restaurantdive.com/spons/recognition-is-the-loyalty-perk-most-restaurants-arent-offering/813866/); [Toast blog](https://pos.toasttab.com/es-us/blog/data/why-some-customers-dont-want-discounts)
- Confusing redemption rules, clunky apps and low-value rewards disengage members — [SMG](https://smg.com/blog/loyalty-programs-what-customers-value-most)
- Enterprise burden: Punchh needs significant internal ownership. Thanx needs marketing staff and charges support and CS line items. Toast loyalty requires Toast POS and is weak at lifecycle marketing — [Restaurant Bottom Line](https://restaurantbottomline.com/2026/08/20/punchh-vs-thanx-vs-toast-loyalty-restaurant-loyalty-2026/)
- Free food is a P&L cost, so operators should agree on a written reward-budget cap — [Restaurant Bottom Line](https://restaurantbottomline.com/2026/08/20/punchh-vs-thanx-vs-toast-loyalty-restaurant-loyalty-2026/)
- Vendor claim: moving loyalty from email or apps to SMS raises redemption 3x to 5x (marketing claim, unverified) — [Boostly](https://www.boostly.com/blog/why-sms-loyalty-programs-work-for-restaurants-(and-outperform-custom-apps))

### Inferences
- "Data siloed in POS" is structural. Toast and Square loyalty and marketing only work for their own POS customers, and enterprise tools need integrations and contracts. An operator who switches POS loses loyalty history unless the platform holds it independently. The Pass already counts visits from bookings plus Square checks, so it owns the guest record across POS.
- The biggest addressable complaint is "can't tell if it works." The Pass can show incremental lift with a holdout group per campaign, in cents.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/restaurateur, r/restaurantowners) threads did not appear in two searches, including a site: query. First-person complaints about 10DLC rejections, carrier filtering, deliverability, data export, generic AI copy and gift cards tied to POS are therefore unsourced here and need a follow-up (direct Reddit search, or G2/Capterra review pages for Toast Marketing, Square Marketing, Popmenu and Owner.com).
- No G2/Capterra review text was pulled, only star ratings.

## Results from best-in-class programs and tactics that work

### Takeaway
Credible numbers are almost all vendor case studies without holdout groups. Birthday programs (47% redemption, +26% birthday-month visits) and win-back ("We Miss You": about 20% response, an 11.5% incremental visit lift where a lift was measured) are the most consistently reported winners for independents. A practical loyalty benchmark: enrolled guests should visit at least 20% more often than non-enrolled guests within 90 days.

### Cited Findings
- Paytronix regional QSR birthday program: 47% redemption, +26% targeted visits in the birthday month, no added staff time — [CaseStudies.com / Paytronix](https://www.casestudies.com/company/paytronix-systems/case-study/regional-quick-serve-restaurant-chain-customer-case-study)
- Paytronix "We Miss You" win-back: about 20% response rate over more than a year. A separate franchisee analysis claims about 10x return on offer cost — [Paytronix PDF](https://www.paytronix.com/hubfs/Case%20Studies/Paytronix_CaseStudy_We_Miss_You.pdf); [Paytronix PDF 2](https://www.paytronix.com/hubfs/Case%20Studies/Paytronix_CaseStudy_We_Miss_You_Lots_Money.pdf)
- Paytronix lifecycle win-back with offers and messaging refreshed quarterly: 11.5% incremental visit lift, 3% spend lift — [Paytronix Lifecycle PDF](https://www.paytronix.com/hubfs/Case%20Studies/CaseStudy_LifecycleCampaigns.pdf)
- OpenTable restaurant group: a "We Miss You" email with the last-visit reminder and 20% off generated $300K+ revenue and 8,500+ covers — [OpenTable](https://restaurant.opentable.com/?p=95832); Garces Group lapsed-guest story — [OpenTable AU](https://restaurant.opentable.com.au/content/customer-stories-parent-page/garces-group-lapsed-guests)
- Denny's with Klaviyo (Roswell Studios): email+SMS about 28% of store revenue within 7 months, email-attributed revenue 9.7x (attribution, not incrementality) — [CaseStudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/roswell-studios/case-study/dennys-drives-28-of-store-revenue-with-roswell-studios)
- Toast: one restaurant attributed $11K+ in sales to SMS in month one — [Toast Q3 2024 call](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2024-11-7-toast-inc-stock)
- Loyalty benchmarks (author's, not vendor data): enrolled guests should visit at least 20% more often than non-enrolled guests within 90 days. Under 15% means the program isn't earning its keep; over 30% is genuine impact. Aim for +0.5 visits per member per quarter. Break-even: at a $25 check and 70% gross margin, Punchh (about $24K/yr) needs about 1,370 extra visits/yr per location (about 7/week), while Toast Loyalty (about $2,220/yr) needs about 130 — [Restaurant Bottom Line](https://restaurantbottomline.com/2026/08/20/punchh-vs-thanx-vs-toast-loyalty-restaurant-loyalty-2026/)
- Timing practice: win-back at 60 to 90 days lapsed (some say 45 to 60). Birthday sent 5 to 7 days ahead and valid two weeks — via search summary of [Paytronix email ideas](https://www.paytronix.com/blog/restaurant-email-marketing-ideas) and [Reelo](https://reelo.io/blog/automating-restaurant-marketing-with-crm-campaign-ideas-that-work/) (attribution per snippet)
- Unverified, low-provenance claims (do not rely on): Noma Street Kitchen SMS repeat rate 18% to 68% ([pages.dev](https://textup-1b1.pages.dev/blog-restaurant-case-study)); Black-eyed Pea +22% repeat ([personal Google Site](https://sites.google.com/view/audikarabi/opscrm/case-zapier))

### Inferences
- For independents, the tactics with evidence are automated birthday, lapsed-regular win-back, and the "last visit" personal reminder. The Reservations Agent's quiet-night texts to lapsed regulars combine win-back with slow-night fill, and the OpenTable "remind them of their last visit" pattern supports that design.
- Few vendors report holdout-based lift. Built-in control groups would be a credible differentiator, and would answer the "40% measure no ROI" complaint.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) study of repeat-visit lift for independents was found.
- No reliable revenue-per-SMS or revenue-per-email benchmark for restaurants was found.
- No data was found specifically on slow-night-fill campaigns or VIP programs.

## Compliance: TCPA, 10DLC, CTIA, CAN-SPAM, gift card laws

### Takeaway
SMS cost is mostly compliance overhead (10DLC brand and campaign registration, vetting, monthly campaign fees, multi-day approval). Gift cards carry a federal 5-year no-expiry floor plus state-by-state escheat rules that vary widely. Sourced detail on TCPA, CTIA and CAN-SPAM was not gathered in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- 10DLC: all Twilio US A2P campaigns are manually vetted ($15 non-refundable), with monthly campaign fees of $1.50 to $10 and up to 5 business days for approval. Brand fee is $4.50 plus $41.50 secondary vetting for Standard brands (post Aug 2025) — [Twilio Vetting FAQ](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/11587910480155-A2P-10DLC-Campaign-Vetting-FAQ); [Twilio support](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260803965530)
- Twilio added new requirements for 10DLC registrations (the blog is undated in results) — [Twilio blog](https://twilio.com/blog/new-requirements-a2p-10dlc-registrations)
- Gift cards, federal: under the 2009 CARD Act, cards cannot expire within 5 years of purchase or last load. Dormancy and usage fees are allowed only after 12 months of inactivity, at most one per month, and must be disclosed. Stricter state laws are not preempted — [NRN (2010, older)](https://nrn.com/casual-dining/new-rules-for-gift-cards-go-into-effect)
- Escheat varies by state. Some states exempt gift cards, some escheat fully, and some exempt cards with no expiry or fees. Dormancy periods range from 2 years (Maine) to 5 years — [Journal of Accountancy (2008, older)](https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/issues/2008/dec/statesbiteintobrokengiftcards/); state chart: [Davis Wright Tremaine gift card chart](https://www.dwt.com/files/Uploads/Documents/Publications/Gift%20Card%20Chart%20(3).pdf) (date unknown)
- Breakage accounting: amounts a state requires to be remitted must be excluded from breakage, and promotional cards are typically excluded — [Baker Tilly](https://bakertilly.com/insights/balancing-act-how-account-restaurant-gift-cards)
- State policy brief on unredeemed gift card value — [Budget and Policy Center, Dec 2023](https://budgetandpolicy.org/resources-tools/2023/12/Gift-Card-Brief-2023-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Pass's gift cards (Stripe, emailed codes, partial redemption) should default to no expiry and no fees. That is the simplest posture and exempts the cards from escheat in several states. Give each card a tracked issue date, last-activity date and selling state, so a breakage/liability report can be produced per state. Promotional cards (bonus cards) must be tracked separately from paid value.
- Consent records plus one-click unsubscribe already cover the core of TCPA/CAN-SPAM. The remaining operator pain is 10DLC paperwork, which The Pass can absorb.

### Gaps
- No sourced 2024–2026 detail was gathered on TCPA (e.g., the FCC one-to-one consent rule status and revocation-of-consent rules effective 2025), CTIA quiet hours or content rules, carrier filtering (SHAFT content, link shorteners), or CAN-SPAM requirements. A follow-up should pull FCC and CTIA primary documents.
- No current statute text for state gift card laws (e.g., California's cash-out for balances under $10) was verified here.

## Concrete gaps The Pass can fill

### Takeaway
The openings are cross-POS guest ownership, measured (holdout) ROI, non-discount recognition loyalty driven by actual visits, done-for-you SMS compliance, slow-night fills from reservation data that no POS-bound tool has, and transparent flat pricing below the Toast-plus-IQ-Grow or Owner.com stack.

### Cited Findings
- Toast loyalty is POS-locked and weak at lifecycle and cohort analysis. Enterprise tools need staff and multi-year contracts — [Restaurant Bottom Line](https://restaurantbottomline.com/2026/08/20/punchh-vs-thanx-vs-toast-loyalty-restaurant-loyalty-2026/)
- Operators find programs too basic (39%) and rarely tiered (16%) — [Bikky 2026](https://bikky.com/blog/bikkys-2026-loyalty-sentiment-survey)
- Recognition is the under-offered perk — [Restaurant Dive (sponsored)](https://www.restaurantdive.com/spons/recognition-is-the-loyalty-perk-most-restaurants-arent-offering/813866/)
- Brands are trying to cut discount levels — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/why-so-many-restaurant-chains-are-revamping-their-loyalty-programs)
- Toast is charging $499 to $699/mo for AI marketing (IQ Grow) on top of the about $185 Essentials — [SaaS Hero](https://www.saashero.net/strategy/toast-iq-grow-marketing-suite/) / Merchant Cost Consulting snippet (unverified)

### Inferences (product opportunities for The Pass)
1. **Holdout-measured lift by default.** Every agent campaign (win-back, birthday, quiet night) automatically withholds a small control group. Report incremental covers and revenue in cents, with `—` when data is missing. This answers "40% measure no ROI" and the 20%/90-day enrolled-vs-not benchmark directly.
2. **Quiet-night fill from reservation and covers data.** POS-bound tools know checks, not tomorrow's empty tables. The Reservations Agent can target lapsed regulars against forecast low covers, with budget caps and an approval queue. Treat this as the flagship differentiator.
3. **Recognition-first loyalty.** Add VIP status from visit frequency (tiers, a host-stand "regular" flag, a chef's comp) alongside visit-count rewards, to limit discount erosion. Enforce a monthly reward-cost cap in the policy check.
4. **Done-for-you 10DLC.** Register brand and campaign under a Twilio ISV setup, pre-fill use case and sample messages, and track approval status in the app. Enforce quiet hours and opt-out language in the policy layer.
5. **POS-independent guest record and export.** Loyalty history and consent survive a POS switch. Offer a one-click CSV export of guests, consent and balances as a trust signal.
6. **Gift card liability reporting.** No-expiry, no-fee defaults. Report outstanding liability, breakage estimates and per-state dormancy. Track promotional cards separately. Gift cards are not tied to any POS.
7. **Non-generic AI copy.** Drafts cite the guest's actual last visit (date, party size, the dish if POS line items exist), in line with the "last visit" pattern from the OpenTable case study, with owner approval.
8. **Transparent SMS economics.** A flat fee with a stated text allowance and pass-through overage, compared against Square's 1.5 to 3¢/text.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence was gathered on whether independents will pay for these specific features, or on churn reasons from Owner.com or Popmenu.
- How well Thanx, SevenRooms and Owner.com handle the "quiet-night fill" use case was not verified. SevenRooms' reservation-aware marketing may already partly overlap.
