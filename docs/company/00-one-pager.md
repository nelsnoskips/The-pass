# The Pass

*A Madison Four company · November 2026*

**One platform for an independent restaurant's dining room and guests —
reservations, guest list, website, SMS and email, loyalty, reviews and
ads — run by software agents that propose, while the owner approves.**

---

### Who it's for

Full-service independent restaurants and small groups (1–20 locations) in
the US. There are about 412,500 independent restaurants
([Restaurant Business / Technomic](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/number-independent-restaurants-declined-23-2025)).
We estimate about half are full-service (an assumption to validate).

### The problem

- **Fees that grow with success.** OpenTable charges $1.00–$1.50 per
  network cover and, since January 2026, 2% on deposits and no-show fees
  ([Eat App](https://restaurant.eatapp.co/blog/opentable-pricing);
  [TableHelm](https://www.tablehelm.com/blog/opentable-2-percent-service-fee-explained)).
- **Lock-in.** OpenTable's 2026 contract requires it to be the "system of
  record" and charges remaining fees for leaving early
  ([Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/opentable-will-require-restaurants-make-it-their-primary-reservations-system)).
- **Too many tools.** A typical full-service stack costs about $1,560 a
  month, or about $2,460 with voice AI and an agency (list prices; see
  the competitive analysis).
- **Someone else owns the guest.** The reservation leaders now belong to
  Booking Holdings, American Express and DoorDash
  ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/doordash-buys-12b-reservation-platform-to-battle-opentable/502986)).

### What we do differently

- **Neutral.** No marketplace, delivery or card business behind us. The
  restaurant owns its guests, accounts and data, and can export
  everything any time.
- **Agents with an approval step.** Agents propose; owners approve
  anything that spends money or messages guests. Every action is logged.
- **Built on who actually sat down.** Booking and visit data makes review
  replies specific, marketing measurable against empty tables, and ad
  spend accountable to seated covers.

**Built today:** reservations engine, host stand, guest list with consent
records, import/export, Square visit sync, and Reservations, Paid Ads,
Website and Reviews agents.
