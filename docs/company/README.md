## Decisions these documents share

- **Wedge:** reservations for full-service independents (1–20 locations),
  entered through a free leak audit, expanding to the full suite.
- **Position:** the neutral platform. No marketplace, delivery or card
  business behind it; the restaurant owns its guests; agents propose and
  owners approve anything that spends money or messages a guest.
- **Prices (hypotheses to test in discovery):** Reservations $199 and the
  full suite $599 per location per month; additional locations 20% off;
  no per-cover, per-order or guest fees and no cut of deposits; 2,000 texts
  a month included; month to month. First 50 restaurants: $149 / $449,
  locked for 24 months.
- **Plan of record for numbers:** the three-year model in 04. Other
  documents quote it.

## Before the first paying restaurant

Found while writing these, and listed as launch blockers in 05 and 06:

1. **Payments go to one platform Stripe account.** Deposits, gift cards and
   orders need Stripe Connect so the money is the restaurant's, before
   "passed through at cost, no cut" is literally true.
2. **Inbound STOP isn't recorded.** Replies to a text must set the guest's
   opt-out, and each restaurant needs its own 10DLC registration and number.
3. **Import keeps notes, visit count and last visit only.** Tags, allergies
   and visit history must come across, and notes must merge, for switch
   kits to keep their promise.
