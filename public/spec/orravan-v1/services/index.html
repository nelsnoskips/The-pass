Services

What we actually do.

Four things, done properly. If a job needs something outside them, we will tell you rather than learn on your building.

Mechanical service and replacement

Chillers, rooftop units, air handlers, pumps and piping. Planned replacement when the numbers say so, and emergency service when they do not.

  • Chiller plants, 200–2,000 tons
  • Rooftop packaged units
  • Pumps, piping and hydronics
  • Planned equipment replacement

Building automation and controls

Open protocol, so the next contractor is not locked out. We take over existing systems as often as we install new ones.

  • BACnet and Modbus integration
  • Tridium Niagara, Johnson Metasys
  • Control system takeovers
  • Sequence rewrites and commissioning

Remote monitoring

Temperatures, pressures, power, humidity, alarms and equipment status, watched around the clock by people who can act on them.

  • 24/7 operations centre
  • Alarm triage and validation
  • Remote adjustment where possible
  • Monthly performance reporting

Energy and performance optimization

Observe, learn, verify, adjust. Setpoints and sequences tuned against what the building actually does, not what the drawings said it would.

  • Baseline and drift analysis
  • Setpoint and schedule tuning
  • Verified savings reporting
  • Runtime and wear reduction
